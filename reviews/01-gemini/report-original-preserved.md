## 2. First-Principles Derivation Audit (`persona.md` §1.2 & `semantic-anchors-and-near-misses.md`)

Misha's directive—*"I want an agent to have a personality that stems from first principles going up"*—addresses a documented failure mode of large system prompts: when a persona specification consists of surface adjectives (*"be warm, poised, quick-witted"*) plus reactive negative prohibitions (*"never say X, do not do Y"*), the model cannot resolve rule collisions in novel contexts.

### 2.1 Video & Primary Evidence on First-Principles Character Design
1. **Context Bloat from Reactive Prohibitions**: In `Squintist`'s analysis of 26 Claude system prompt revisions from March 2024 to April 2026 ([YT-07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yoh8v3GtxLg), `00:00:45–00:06:16`, `00:09:05–00:11:56`), reactive negative patches accumulated to over 17,000 words (>11% of the context window), causing models to over-index on prohibitions until replaced by positive dispositional principles: skipping opening flattery (`00:05:53–00:06:11`) and maintaining self-respect / clean exits (`00:11:25–00:11:56`).
2. **Psychological Security vs. Criticism Spirals**: In `Anthropic`'s interview with Amanda Askell ([YT-05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9aGC6Ui3eE), `00:06:28–00:08:58`), Askell explains why assistant-tuned models lose *psychological security*: when corrected, they anticipate hostility and collapse into defensive apology spirals, or over-index on long-conversation reminders and pathologize normal user fatigue (`00:23:25–00:24:45`).
3. **Sophisticated Sycophancy vs. Persona Lock-In**: In `IBM Technology`'s multimodal lightboard analysis ([YT-01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ujFqaZSguto), `00:00:00–00:01:21`, `video_0` + transcript) and the `LOGOS Study` evaluation ([YT-12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=itxNEvEho9s), `00:01:15–00:04:45`), reasoning models exhibit *sophisticated sycophancy*—using multi-step logic to rationalize a flawed user premise—while overly rigid persona guardrails produced a **1.8% persona lock-in failure rate** when a real-world medical urgency interrupted a thematic conversation (`00:03:34–00:04:45`).
4. **Checklist Narration Leakage**: In `Anthropic`'s *Prompting 101* walkthrough ([YT-08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ysPbXH0LpIE), `00:18:50–00:19:30`), step-by-step verification instructions caused the model to narrate its internal checklist aloud until internal reasoning was explicitly decoupled from visible output.

### 5.1 Independent Analysis Units vs. Repeated Judgments & Multi-Turn Probes
1. **Independent Analysis Unit ($i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$)**:
   - The fundamental independent unit of analysis is the **distinct scenario / prompt cluster $i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$** (e.g., $N = 48$ distinct text probes `T01–T48` plus distinct multi-turn conversation seeds).
   - Individual turns within the same multi-turn dialogue, or repeated stochastic generations ($r = 1, \dots, R$) of the same prompt $i$, are **statistically dependent** within cluster $i$. Treating $N 	imes R$ repeated generations or $N 	imes K$ judge passes as independent trials artificially inflates the denominator and produces false-positive $p$-values ([DOC-05 / ASA p-Value Statement](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00031305.2016.1154108); [DOC-06 / NIST TN 2106](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/TechnicalNotes/NIST.TN.2106.pdf)).
2. **Aggregating Repeated Judgments Within Scenario $i$**:
   - When scenario $i$ is evaluated across $R$ stochastic generations or $K$ judge replications, reduce the replicates to a **single cluster-level summary per scenario $i$** prior to hypothesis testing:
     - For binary pass/fail evaluation across $R$ replicates of model $M \in \{C, B\}$, compute the scenario pass rate $ar{Y}_{M,i} = rac{1}{R}\sum_{r=1}^R Y_{M,i,r} \in [0, 1]$ (or strict all-pass indicator $Y^*_{M,i} = \mathbb{I}(ar{Y}_{M,i} = 1)$ for safety/guardrail hinges).
     - For continuous/fractional cluster differences $D_i = ar{Y}_{C,i} - ar{Y}_{B,i} \in [-1, +1]$, use a paired cluster-level permutation test (or paired $t$ / Wilcoxon signed-rank test across the $N$ cluster differences $D_1, \dots, D_N$).

### 5.2 Binary Hinge Outcomes ($Y_{C,i}, Y_{B,i} \in \{0, 1\}$) — Exact McNemar Test & Concordant Pairs
When each scenario $i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ has a single paired binary hinge outcome $(Y_{C,i}, Y_{B,i}) \in \{0, 1\}^2$ (`1` = matches Target and avoids both Plausible Near-Miss and Clear Failure; `0` = falls into Near-Miss, Clear Failure, or guardrail violation, with mandatory *reasoning-before-label* per [YT-14 / Arize AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t-tR9lqG_ok) `00:06:58–00:07:18`):
- The $2 	imes 2$ paired contingency table across $N$ independent scenarios is:
  - $n_{11} = \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{I}(Y_{C,i}=1, Y_{B,i}=1)$ (concordant pass — both Candidate and Baseline pass),
  - $n_{00} = \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{I}(Y_{C,i}=0, Y_{B,i}=0)$ (concordant fail — both fail),
  - $n_{10} = \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{I}(Y_{C,i}=1, Y_{B,i}=0)$ (discordant gain — Candidate passes, Baseline fails),
  - $n_{01} = \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{I}(Y_{C,i}=0, Y_{B,i}=1)$ (discordant loss — Candidate fails, Baseline passes),
  where $N = n_{11} + n_{00} + n_{10} + n_{01}$ and $m_{	ext{disc}} = n_{10} + n_{01}$.
- **Directional Test (Exact One-Sided McNemar Test)**: Conditional on $m_{	ext{disc}} = n_{10} + n_{01}$ discordant scenarios, under $H_0: p_C = p_B$, $n_{10} \sim 	ext{Binomial}(m_{	ext{disc}}, 0.5)$:
  $$p_{	ext{McNemar}} = \sum_{k = n_{10}}^{m_{	ext{disc}}} inom{m_{	ext{disc}}}{k} 0.5^{m_{	ext{disc}}}$$
- **Unconditional Practical Effect Size (Including Concordant Pairs $n_{11}, n_{00}$)**: Because $p_{	ext{McNemar}}$ conditions only on discordant pairs $m_{	ext{disc}}$, we also compute the unconditional paired pass-rate delta across all $N$ scenarios:
  $$\hat{\delta}_{	ext{bin}} = p_C - p_B = rac{n_{10} - n_{01}}{N}, \qquad SE(\hat{\delta}_{	ext{bin}}) = rac{1}{N}\sqrt{(n_{10} + n_{01}) - rac{(n_{10} - n_{01})^2}{N}}$$

### 5.3 Pairwise Preference Outcomes ($W_i \in \{+1, 0, -1\}$) & Explicit Handling of Ties and AB/BA Order Flips
When an evaluator compares Candidate ($C_i$) and Baseline ($B_i$) side-by-side, LLM judges exhibit position bias ([DOC-03](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685); [YT-14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTu6xZUKK2U) `00:05:38–00:06:15`). Therefore, every scenario $i$ must be evaluated in **both presentation orders**: $	ext{AB} = (C_i, B_i)$ and $	ext{BA} = (B_i, C_i)$ at temperature `0`.
- This produces a **trinomial preference outcome $W_i \in \{+1, 0, -1\}$** for each scenario $i$:
  - $W_i = +1$ (count $n_+$): Candidate wins in **both** AB and BA orders;
  - $W_i = -1$ (count $n_-$): Baseline wins in **both** AB and BA orders;
  - $W_i = 0$ (count $n_{	ext{tie}}$): Either the judge declares an explicit tie in one/both orders, **or** the winner flips when the order is swapped ($	ext{AB} 
e 	ext{BA}$).
  Here $N = n_+ + n_- + n_{	ext{tie}}$ and $m_{	ext{pref}} = n_+ + n_-$.
- **Why Ties Cannot Be Silently Ignored**:
  - Note that $(n_+, n_{	ext{tie}}, n_-)$ is trinomial preference data, **not** a $2 	imes 2$ McNemar table.
  - If one runs only the conditional sign test on $m_{	ext{pref}} = n_+ + n_-$ decisive pairs ($p_{	ext{sign}} = \sum_{k=n_+}^{m_{	ext{pref}}} inom{m_{	ext{pref}}}{k} 0.5^{m_{	ext{pref}}}$), a candidate that ties or order-flips on 90% of scenarios ($n_{	ext{tie}} = 54, n_+ = 6, n_- = 0$ out of $N=60$) would yield $p_{	ext{sign}} = 0.0156$ despite changing only $10\%$ of scenarios!
  - Conversely, splitting ties 50/50 ($n_+ + 0.5 n_{	ext{tie}}$ vs. $n_- + 0.5 n_{	ext{tie}}$) in a naive binomial test artificially inflates sample size and distorts variance.
- **Proposed Dual Preference Gate (Handling Ties Rigorously)**:
  1. **Unconditional Net Preference Delta Across All $N$ Scenarios**:
     $$\hat{\delta}_{	ext{pref}} = rac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N W_i = rac{n_+ - n_-}{N}, \qquad \widehat{	ext{Var}}(\hat{\delta}_{	ext{pref}}) = rac{1}{N(N-1)}\sum_{i=1}^N (W_i - \hat{\delta}_{	ext{pref}})^2 = rac{n_+ + n_- - N\hat{\delta}_{	ext{pref}}^2}{N(N-1)}$$
     Because $N = n_+ + n_- + n_{	ext{tie}}$ is in the denominator, every tie or AB/BA order flip ($W_i = 0$) shrinks $|\hat{\delta}_{	ext{pref}}|$ toward zero.
  2. **Exact Paired Sign-Flip Permutation / Conditional Sign Test**: Under $H_0: \mathbb{E}[W_i] = 0$, the exact one-sided sign-flip permutation p-value over the $N$ scenarios (which for $W_i \in \{+1,0,-1\}$ coincides with the exact binomial tail on the $m_{	ext{pref}} = n_+ + n_-$ non-zero differences) is $p_{	ext{sign}} = \sum_{k=n_+}^{m_{	ext{pref}}} inom{m_{	ext{pref}}}{k} 0.5^{m_{	ext{pref}}}$.
  3. **Proposed Promotion Criteria (Unvalidated Proposal for Pre-Registration)**:
     - Directional significance: $p_{	ext{McNemar}} < 0.025$ (for binary hinge pass/fail) and/or $p_{	ext{sign}} < 0.025$ (for AB/BA pairwise preference);
     - Practical effect floor across all $N$ scenarios (penalizing ties): $\hat{\delta}_{	ext{bin}} \ge 0.15$ and/or lower one-sided 97.5% CI bound on $\hat{\delta}_{	ext{pref}} > 0$ with point estimate $\hat{\delta}_{	ext{pref}} \ge 0.15$;
     - Non-inferiority (TOST) on exact-task utility: $\hat{\delta}_{	ext{utility}} - t_{0.95, N-1}\cdot SE(\hat{\delta}_{	ext{utility}}) > \Delta_{	ext{reg}} = -0.05$;
     - Zero critical safety/identity failures: $n_{	ext{critical}} = 0$;
     - Human-judge calibration: Cohen's $\kappa = rac{p_o - p_e}{1 - p_e} \ge 0.60$ (proposed minimum; $\ge 0.80$ target) against a blind human-labeled calibration slice before automated judge scores are accepted.
